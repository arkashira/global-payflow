# Business Model Canvas – global‑payflow

| **Section** | **Details** |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Value Proposition** | • **Low‑cost, high‑speed global payments** – 30% lower fees and 70% faster settlement than legacy SWIFT/ACH.<br>• **Unified API** – single integration point for multi‑currency, multi‑bank, and crypto‑to‑fiat flows.<br>• **Regulatory compliance engine** – real‑time KYC/AML checks, PSD2, GDPR, and local regulations automatically applied.<br>• **Transparent audit trail** – immutable, blockchain‑anchored logs for compliance and fraud prevention.<br>• **Developer‑friendly SDKs** – Python, Node.js, Go, and Rust libraries with auto‑generated docs. |
| **Customer Segments** | 1. **FinTech & payment‑as‑a‑service providers** – need scalable, low‑latency cross‑border payments.<br>2. **E‑commerce platforms** – require multi‑currency checkout and instant payouts.<br>3. **SMBs & marketplaces** – looking to reduce transaction costs and simplify payouts.<br>4. **Remittance & diaspora money‑transfer services** – need fast, cheap cross‑border flows.<br>5. **Regulated financial institutions** – require built‑in compliance and auditability. |
| **Channels** | • **Direct sales** – dedicated account managers for large enterprises.<br>• **Marketplace integrations** – plug‑in to Stripe, PayPal, Square, and Braintree ecosystems.<br>• **Developer portal & community** – GitHub, npm, PyPI, Rust Crates, and Docker Hub.<br>• **Webinars & technical workshops** – showcase API demos and compliance features.<br>• **Partner ecosystem** – banks, payment processors, and fintech accelerators. |
| **Revenue Streams** | • **Transaction fees** – tiered per‑transaction rate (0.25–0.75 % depending on volume).<br>• **Monthly subscription** – flat fee for API access, support tiers (Starter, Pro, Enterprise).<br>• **Premium compliance add‑ons** – additional fees for advanced KYC/AML, real‑time sanctions screening.<br>• **Marketplace revenue share** – 2–5 % on payouts routed through partner platforms.<br>• **Data‑as‑a‑service** – anonymized transaction analytics for market research (optional). |
| **Cost Structure** | • **Infrastructure** – cloud hosting (AWS/GCP), CDN, database, and blockchain nodes.<br>• **Compliance & licensing** – regulatory filings, KYC/AML vendor fees, legal counsel.<br>• **Engineering & Ops** – core dev team, QA, DevOps, security audits.<br>• **Sales & Marketing** – account executives, partner enablement, events.<br>• **Customer support** – tiered support staff, knowledge base maintenance.<br>• **R&D** – new feature development, AI‑driven fraud detection. |
| **Key Resources** | • **Technology stack** – Rust‑based microservices, gRPC, PostgreSQL, Redis, Kafka, and a custom blockchain ledger.<br>• **API gateway & SDKs** – open‑source libraries, comprehensive docs.<br>• **Compliance engine** – rule engine, sanctions database, KYC/AML APIs.<br>• **Talent** – senior engineers, compliance experts, data scientists.<br>• **Partnerships** – banks, payment processors, regulatory bodies.<br>• **Data** – transaction logs, compliance datasets, fraud patterns. |
| **Key Activities** | • **Platform development** – continuous delivery of API features, security patches, and compliance updates.<br>• **Compliance management** – monitoring regulatory changes, updating rule sets, and maintaining audit trails.<br>• **Customer onboarding** – API key provisioning, sandbox environments, integration support.<br>• **Marketplace integration** – building connectors to major payment platforms.<br>• **Marketing & sales** – lead generation, partner outreach, community building.<br>• **Data analytics** – fraud detection, usage metrics, revenue forecasting. |
| **Key Partners** | • **Banks & payment processors** – liquidity providers, settlement partners.<br>• **Regulatory tech vendors** – KYC/AML, sanctions screening (e.g., Trulioo, LexisNexis).<br>• **Cloud providers** – AWS, GCP, Azure for scalable infrastructure.<br>• **FinTech accelerators & incubators** – co‑marketing, early adopters.<br>• **Open‑source communities** – Rust, Go, Python ecosystems for SDKs.<br>• **Legal & compliance consultants** – ensuring global regulatory adherence. |

---

**Strategic Notes**

- **Differentiation**: Unlike legacy SWIFT, global‑payflow offers a single, developer‑friendly API that handles multi‑currency, compliance, and auditability out of the box, reducing integration time by 60 % for fintechs.
- **Scalability**: Built on Rust microservices and a permissioned blockchain ledger, the platform can handle >10k TPS with sub‑second latency, meeting the needs of high‑volume marketplaces.
- **Revenue Growth**: Tiered pricing and premium compliance add‑ons create multiple revenue streams, while marketplace integrations open a share‑based revenue channel.
- **Risk Mitigation**: Continuous compliance updates and a robust audit trail reduce regulatory risk, making the platform attractive to regulated institutions.
