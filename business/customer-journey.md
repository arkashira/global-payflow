# Customer Journey Map for Global Payflow

## 1. Aware
- **Trigger Event**: A bank or payment provider experiences delays and high costs in processing international transactions.
- **Friction Points**: Lack of awareness about alternative payment infrastructures; existing solutions are perceived as outdated or inefficient.
- **User Emotions**: Frustration, confusion, and urgency to find a solution.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Educational content (webinars, whitepapers) highlighting the benefits of modern payment infrastructures; case studies showcasing successful implementations.
- **Metric**: Number of leads generated through awareness campaigns (e.g., downloads of educational content).

## 2. Consider
- **Trigger Event**: Users start researching payment infrastructure solutions after identifying inefficiencies in their current systems.
- **Friction Points**: Overwhelming number of options; difficulty in comparing features and costs; skepticism about claims made by providers.
- **User Emotions**: Cautious optimism mixed with anxiety about making the wrong choice.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Interactive comparison tools; personalized consultations to address specific needs; transparent pricing models.
- **Metric**: Engagement rate with comparison tools and consultation requests.

## 3. Try
- **Trigger Event**: Users sign up for a free trial or demo of Global Payflow.
- **Friction Points**: Complicated onboarding process; lack of immediate support during trial; integration challenges with existing systems.
- **User Emotions**: Hopeful but apprehensive about the trial experience.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Streamlined onboarding with guided tutorials; proactive customer support during the trial; easy integration with existing systems.
- **Metric**: Trial conversion rate (percentage of users who convert to paid after trial).

## 4. Adopt
- **Trigger Event**: Users decide to implement Global Payflow as their primary payment infrastructure.
- **Friction Points**: Resistance to change from internal stakeholders; potential downtime during transition; concerns about security and compliance.
- **User Emotions**: Excitement mixed with anxiety about the transition process.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Comprehensive support during the transition; robust security assurances; success stories from similar organizations.
- **Metric**: Time taken to fully implement the solution and user satisfaction scores post-implementation.

## 5. Expand
- **Trigger Event**: Users experience improved efficiency and cost savings, prompting them to explore additional features or services.
- **Friction Points**: Limited awareness of all available features; potential for feature overload; concerns about ongoing costs.
- **User Emotions**: Satisfaction with current performance but curiosity about maximizing value.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Regular check-ins to discuss additional features; tailored recommendations based on usage patterns; loyalty rewards for expanding services.
- **Metric**: Rate of upsells or cross-sells of additional features/services.
