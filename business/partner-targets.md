## Partner Integration Roadmap for Global Payflow

### Target Integrations

1. **Stripe**
   - **Rationale**: Widely used payment processing platform that supports a variety of payment methods globally. Integrating with Stripe allows Global Payflow to leverage its extensive network and user base.
   - **Free-Tier Limits**: No free tier; transaction fees apply.
   - **Integration Effort**: Medium (M)
   - **Value-Add**: Simplifies payment processing for businesses, enabling them to accept payments quickly and efficiently.

2. **PayPal**
   - **Rationale**: A leading online payment system that supports global transactions. Integration with PayPal enhances trust and accessibility for users.
   - **Free-Tier Limits**: No free tier; transaction fees apply.
   - **Integration Effort**: Medium (M)
   - **Value-Add**: Facilitates easy payments for consumers and businesses, addressing the need for a trusted payment method.

3. **Square**
   - **Rationale**: Popular among small to medium-sized businesses for payment processing. Integrating with Square can attract a segment of the market focused on retail and e-commerce.
   - **Free-Tier Limits**: No free tier; transaction fees apply.
   - **Integration Effort**: Medium (M)
   - **Value-Add**: Provides a comprehensive payment solution that includes point-of-sale capabilities, enhancing user experience.

4. **Adyen**
   - **Rationale**: A global payment company that allows businesses to accept payments in multiple currencies. Integration with Adyen can enhance Global Payflow's international capabilities.
   - **Free-Tier Limits**: No free tier; transaction fees apply.
   - **Integration Effort**: Large (L)
   - **Value-Add**: Streamlines international transactions, reducing costs and processing times for users.

5. **Braintree**
   - **Rationale**: A PayPal service that offers a seamless payment experience for mobile and web applications. Integrating with Braintree can enhance mobile payment capabilities.
   - **Free-Tier Limits**: No free tier; transaction fees apply.
   - **Integration Effort**: Medium (M)
   - **Value-Add**: Simplifies mobile payments, addressing the growing demand for mobile commerce solutions.

6. **Plaid**
   - **Rationale**: Provides a secure way to connect bank accounts to applications, facilitating ACH payments. Integrating with Plaid can enhance the payment infrastructure for bank transfers.
   - **Free-Tier Limits**: Free tier available for up to 100 transactions/month.
   - **Integration Effort**: Medium (M)
   - **Value-Add**: Enables seamless bank transfers, addressing the need for low-cost payment options.

7. **Coinbase Commerce**
   - **Rationale**: Allows merchants to accept cryptocurrency payments. Integration with Coinbase Commerce can attract businesses looking to accept digital assets.
   - **Free-Tier Limits**: No free tier; transaction fees apply.
   - **Integration Effort**: Medium (M)
   - **Value-Add**: Expands payment options for businesses, addressing the growing interest in cryptocurrency transactions.

8. **Authorize.Net**
   - **Rationale**: A long-standing payment gateway that provides a reliable solution for online payments. Integrating with Authorize.Net can enhance credibility and trust for users.
   - **Free-Tier Limits**: No free tier; transaction fees apply.
   - **Integration Effort**: Medium (M)
   - **Value-Add**: Offers a robust payment processing solution, addressing the needs of businesses looking for reliability.

### Summary
The proposed integrations focus on enhancing Global Payflow's capabilities in the payment infrastructure space, addressing critical user jobs such as simplifying payment processing, enabling international transactions, and expanding payment options. Prioritizing partnerships with revenue-share opportunities will further enhance the product's value proposition and revenue potential.
